Cosmetics Asia is an informational and travel-coordination companion for adults considering cosmetic care in Asia.

No account is required. All primary tabs are available immediately after launch. The care-plan checklist is stored locally on the device and is not uploaded to Cosmetics Asia. External contact actions are user initiated and open the device's chosen communication service.

The app is not a medical device, does not diagnose or recommend treatment, and does not replace a licensed healthcare professional. Published profiles and directory entries are clearly presented as information rather than rankings or endorsements.

Suggested review path:
1. Open Experts and view a published profile.
2. Open Clinics and search for Shanghai.
3. Open Diaries and play a journey preview.
4. Open My plan, select a procedure and destination, complete one checklist item, then use Share my plan.
