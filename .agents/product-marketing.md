# Cosmetics Asia product marketing context

## Product

Cosmetics Asia helps international patients understand cosmetic, dental, and wellness treatment options in China, compare published provider information, and coordinate the practical steps around care.

## Primary audience

International patients who are considering treatment in China but need help understanding providers, costs, language support, travel logistics, and what happens after they return home.

## Primary conversion

Get a free quote. Visitors should feel comfortable beginning with questions even if they have not chosen a procedure, expert, city, budget, or travel date.

## Job to be done

- Understand possible procedures and realistic next steps.
- Compare published provider information without feeling pressured to choose.
- Clarify likely costs and who receives each payment.
- Reduce uncertainty about translation, airport pickup, travel planning, and follow-up.
- Connect with a suitable provider when ready.

## Offer and payment facts

- The initial coordination conversation is free and carries no obligation.
- Medical fees are paid directly to the treating clinic or hospital.
- Cosmetics Asia collects a $400 coordination deposit to reserve the procedure appointment and coordinate airport pickup and in-clinic translation.
- The deposit remains valid for 12 months and is refunded when the patient pays the clinic for treatment.

## Primary objections

- Can I trust the provider information?
- How will I communicate with the clinic?
- What will I pay, and who receives each payment?
- Who coordinates travel and appointment details?
- What support is available after I return home?
- Can I ask questions before I know exactly what I want?

## Voice

Calm, plain, transparent, respectful, and specific. Help visitors make an informed decision without pressure. Prefer short sentences and action-specific labels.

## Trust constraints

- Never describe a provider as verified unless current supporting data exists.
- Clearly identify sample or fictional profiles.
- Never invent testimonials, outcomes, response times, urgency, scarcity, or guarantees.
- Do not provide medical advice or imply that coordination replaces clinical evaluation.
- State fees, inclusions, exclusions, and payment recipients plainly.

## Traffic assumptions

Visitors likely arrive through organic search, social video, patient recovery content, or referrals. Many will enter on a treatment or provider page rather than the homepage, so the free-quote next step should be understandable wherever they land.
